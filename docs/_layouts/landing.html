macOS · PhysioNet WFDB · research use

A microscope and a telescope for ECG waveform data.

Open a PhysioNet/WFDB recording and range from the whole record down to a single heartbeat on calibrated ECG paper. The free viewer renders the signal with its annotation layers and a confirm/dismiss review workflow; a single Murmur Pro purchase unlocks the analysis instruments — beat calipers, whole-record trend lanes, variability metrics, a ranked arrhythmia review queue, and morphology clustering.

The viewer

FREE
  • Import WFDB records — .hea + .dat, multi-frequency included
  • Calibrated ECG paper with the gain / speed seam
  • The record’s own annotator layer, on trace and in queue
  • Confirm / dismiss / reset review dispositions
  • Low-rate trend strips: vitals, alarms, signal quality

Murmur Pro

ONE PURCHASE
